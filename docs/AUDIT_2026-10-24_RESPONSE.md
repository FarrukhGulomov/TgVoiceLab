in the source, what changed, and which test covers it. Everything here is verified offline against fakes and a real PostgreSQL; none of it is live-verified.

## What you must do before relying on this

1. The call path changed (and the fingerprint definition did), so re-run `pnpm evidence:production` and re-record the live call for the exact deployed build.
2. Set `AI_VOICE_COST_USD_PER_MINUTE` to your provider's real cost, otherwise every connected call books its full reservation.
3. Decide F-12 (deterministic introduction / consent basis) and the legal side of F-11.
