## How calling is held off (defense in depth)

1. `CALL_ADAPTER=disabled` and `CALLS_ENABLED=false` by default; `loadConfig` refuses `CALLS_ENABLED=true` without a real adapter, evidence path, budget and owner.
2. `CALL_ADAPTER=ntgcalls` loads the real adapter (requires `MESSAGING_ADAPTER=telegram`) but wraps it in `EvidenceGatedCallAdapter`: it dials only if the evidence artifact (bound to this build, `producedBy: "production-adapter"`) validates. The operator-run spike evidence cannot satisfy that.
3. Capabilities are _derived_ from an evidence artifact bound to the build fingerprint and `producedBy: "production-adapter"`. Spike evidence, mock/synthetic results, a different build, or any missing/invalid gate yields all-false.
4. The durable kill switch defaults **ON** for every new owner and is read inside the dial transaction.
5. Dial policy (`evaluateDialPolicy`) is deterministic and re-evaluated after the call row is taken and before the external side effect.
6. Contact calls additionally require a live-verified local consent recognizer (none exists).
7. `/v1/capabilities` reports `degraded_text_only` with the reasons.

## Meetings through contact calls

The owner can offer meeting times ("Javlonga qo'ng'iroq qilib uchrashuv taklif qil: 15:00 yoki 17:00", or `meeting_slots` in the voice
tool `call_contact`). The offered times are sealed in the approved plan (`allowedContext`, `slot:` entries). On the call the AI may confirm
ONLY one of them (`submit_report` outcome `meeting_agreed` + `agreed_slot`; any other value is refused); anything else is reported as
`meeting_countered` with the contact's own words and commits nothing; `callback_requested` tells the owner the contact asked to be called.
After an agreement the system creates the owner's task ("Uchrashuv: <contact>", due at the slot) and the report states what was really
committed. Live-verified for `meeting_agreed` and `meeting_countered` (see above); contact calls stay gated by the six evidence gates.

## Free-form text (ADR 0005 update)

`GeminiTextFallback` understands text the rules do not recognise (also transcribed voice notes), using contacts and the latest call report as
context. A call it proposes asks for "ha" first. Offline-tested with fakes; **not live verified**.

## Owner memory

`MemoryService` (`memories` table, encrypted, scoped `owner:notes` / `contact:<id>:notes`, owner-approved only, expiry + per-scope cap of 60). The
owner says «eslab qol: …», «xotira», «unut 2» (or the free-form fallback proposes `remember`). In contact calls the AI gets only THAT
contact's notes (as data) and may return 0-5 `notes` with its report, which are stored as contact memory with provenance `call_report`.
Removing a contact forgets their notes. The free-form fallback also sees the owner's notes and notes about contacts named in the message.
Offline-tested; not live verified.

## Replies from contacts

A private message from a vouched, attested contact to the assistant account is relayed to the owner as quoted text (data, never a command;
voice/files are only reported; max 20 relays per contact per hour; strangers are ignored). «X javob bersa menga qo'ng'iroq qilib ayt» sets a
one-shot 48 h `call` watch: the next reply also rings the owner through the reminder-call path (kill switch, quiet hours and opt-out apply).
Offline-tested; not live verified.

## Redial and text fallback

Owner's standing instruction: a contact call that ends as `transport_error` (the contact answered but the audio never connected) is redialled
automatically 45 s later, up to 3 attempts in all (`call_requests.attempt` / `retry_of`). After the third failure the AI sends the contact a
short text (phrased by the text model from the owner's brief, opening with "I am <owner>'s AI assistant") and tells the owner what it sent;
without a text model the owner is told how to send it. No answer, busy, declined and privacy-restricted calls are never redialled. A
voice message instead of text is not built (needs speech synthesis).

## Voice notes

`GeminiTranscription` (generateContent, inline audio) turns owner voice notes into text when `GEMINI_API_KEY` and
`GEMINI_TRANSCRIPTION_MODEL` are set. **Not live verified.** Unsure transcripts are echoed back for confirmation, never acted on.
